Question 1 of 25 2 marks
A simply supported beam of span 8 m carries a uniformly distributed load of 20 kN/m over its entire length. The maximum bending moment in the beam will be:
A
120 kN-m
B
160 kN-m
C
180 kN-m
D
200 kN-m
✓ Correct Answer: B (160 kN-m)
For a simply supported beam with uniformly distributed load (UDL), the maximum bending moment occurs at the center and is given by: M = wL²/8

Where: w = 20 kN/m, L = 8 m
M = (20 × 8²)/8 = (20 × 64)/8 = 160 kN-m
